---

Click "New issue" on
[GitHub](https://github.com/emmaglorypraise/ObsidianLog/issues) and
you'll be offered three templates, one for each of the sections below.

## Giving feedback

Tried ObsidianLog? We'd genuinely like to know how it went, even
briefly, good or bad. Use the **"Tried ObsidianLog? Tell us about it"**
template. This one isn't just for problems. If you got partway through a
[tutorial](/tutorials/local-backend) and got stuck, or something was
confusing rather than broken, that's worth reporting here too, even
without a precise, reproducible bug.

## Reporting a bug

Something didn't work the way it should have? Use the **Bug report**
template. Include what you ran, what you expected, and what happened
instead. `obsidianlog --version` and your OS/platform help a lot. If you
already opened a feedback report for the same problem, it's fine to open
a bug report too and link it from the feedback one, they serve different
purposes.

## Suggesting a feature

Have an idea or enhancement you'd like to see? Use the **Feature
request** template.

## Reporting a security vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities **privately** instead of through a public
issue. See [Security Model](/architecture-and-security/security-model)
for how.

## Questions and discussion

[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/emmaglorypraise/ObsidianLog/discussions)
is the place for open-ended questions about ObsidianLog itself that
aren't quite a bug, a feature request, or feedback.

## Contributing

See [Contributing](/project/contributing) for the development setup, PR
checklist, and conventions.
