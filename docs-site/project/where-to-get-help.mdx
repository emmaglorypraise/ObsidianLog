## Opening an issue

Click "New issue" on
[GitHub](https://github.com/emmaglorypraise/ObsidianLog/issues) and
you'll be offered three templates. Pick whichever fits:

- **Bug report**: something didn't work the way it should have. Include
  what you ran, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  `obsidianlog --version` and your OS/platform help a lot.
- **Feature request**: an idea or enhancement you'd like to see.
- **Tried ObsidianLog? Tell me about it**: general feedback from trying
  it out. This one isn't just for bugs. If you got partway through a
  tutorial and got stuck, that's worth reporting here even without a
  precise, reproducible bug. If you also have reproducible bug details,
  it's fine to open a bug report too and link it from the feedback one.

Found a security vulnerability? See
[Security Model](/architecture-and-security/security-model) and report it
privately. Please don't open a public issue for those.

## Questions and discussion

- **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/emmaglorypraise/ObsidianLog/discussions)**
  for open-ended questions about ObsidianLog itself that aren't quite a
  bug or feature request.
- **[Sia](https://sia.tech)'s community channels** for anything about the
  underlying Sia network, `indexd`, or storage and wallet questions not
  specific to ObsidianLog itself.
- **The Vector community** for questions about shipping logs into
  ObsidianLog via [Vector](https://vector.dev).
